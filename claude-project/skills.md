# [Company] GTM Assistant — Rep Skills

Share this with your sales team. Copy it into Slack, Notion, or wherever your team lives.

Reps open the **[Company] GTM Assistant** Project in Claude.ai and use these three prompts. No repo access needed.

---

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[Company] GTM Assistant — 3 prompts for everything
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Open the [Company] GTM Assistant Project in Claude.ai, then use any of these:


── RESEARCH ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Use before any outreach or discovery call.

  Research [company.com]

Produces: company snapshot, recent hires, tech stack, stakeholder
map (who to contact and how), active signals, competitive context,
and a specific outreach angle with a suggested first line.

Example: Research stripe.com


── PROSPECT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Use before building your weekly list. Paste up to 50 accounts.

  Score these accounts and tell me who to prioritize:
  [paste company names or domains, one per line]

Produces: ICP tier (1–4), total score, qualifying reasons,
and recommended next action for each account. Tier 1 accounts
(immediate outreach) flagged at the top.

Example:
  Score these accounts and tell me who to prioritize:
  Linear, Retool, Rippling, Lattice, Notion, Monday.com


── MESSAGE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Use when you need copy for a specific account + trigger.

  Write messaging for [persona title] at [company name].
  Signal: [what happened]

Produces: 3 subject line options, first touch email, LinkedIn
connection request note, LinkedIn DM (post-connection), voicemail
script, 2 follow-up emails, and a break-up email. All paste-ready.

Example:
  Write messaging for the Head of RevOps at Linear.
  Signal: Series B announced 3 weeks ago, 2 RevOps roles just posted.

For competitive accounts, add to your prompt:
  They currently use [Competitor]. Don't name them in the first touch.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```
